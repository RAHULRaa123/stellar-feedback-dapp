# 🚀 Stellar Trust Network - Product Updates

## Overview

Stellar Trust Network evolved from a simple feedback dApp into a decentralized business trust infrastructure built on Stellar.

The platform focuses on business verification, reputation management, customer reviews, and transparent transaction workflows.

---

# Major Product Improvements

## 1. Business Trust Platform

Added product positioning around:

- Business identity verification
- Reputation scoring
- Customer reviews
- Trust badges
- Business discovery

---

## 2. Blockchain Integration

Implemented:

- Stellar Testnet integration
- Soroban smart contracts
- On-chain feedback storage
- Smart contract interaction flows
- Reward mechanism

---

## 3. User Experience Improvements

Based on user testing:

Initial feedback:
- Loading states unclear
- Mobile experience needed improvement

Implemented:

- Improved loading states
- Better responsive design
- Enhanced user flow

---

## 4. Platform Modules

Current ecosystem includes:

- Business profiles
- Reputation system
- Review management
- Analytics dashboard
- Escrow workflow concept
- Reward system

---

## 5. Deployment & Reliability

Implemented:

- Vercel deployment

- Automated testing
- Production-ready workflow

Building a decentralized trust layer where businesses and customers can interact through verified reputation and transparent blockchain transactions.
